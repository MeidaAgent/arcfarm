CHAPTER I · THE WORN ENVELOPE
ARC 1994 · EXPRESS

You stare out through the rain-streaked window of your high-rise city apartment. Endless car horns, cold concrete towers, and months of soul-draining office hours have dimmed the spark inside you.

Then, a sharp knock echoes at your door. An elderly mailman hands you an old parchment envelope, sealed with crimson beeswax. As your fingers break the wax seal, a faint, sweet fragrance of dried kenanga flowers drifts out. It is the unmistakable handwriting of your grandfather:

"My dearest grandchild,

If you are reading these lines, it means the city's relentless gears have worn you thin. I know that exhausted hollow in your chest, because fifty years ago, I felt the exact same way.

Back then, I walked away from the smog and discovered a hidden sanctuary tucked between misty emerald hills: Kenanga Valley. Here, the river waters run crystal clear, the morning air heals the tired mind, and the soil gives back tenfold whatever love you pour into it.

I cleared the stones, built our family homestead, and lived the happiest years of my life. But time moves on, my hands grew old, and now the fields sleep beneath wild grass and forgotten memories.

I have deeded the entire farm to you. Pack your bag. Leave the concrete behind. Take the morning train to the end of the line. Your true life is waiting on the hill."

— With love, Grandpa

CHAPTER III · THE CYCLE OF FOUR SEASONS

Life in Kenanga Valley follows the ancient calendar of nature. Four distinct 28-day seasons test your farming skills, reward your dedication, and unlock rare secrets:

🌸 SPRING · REBIRTH

Gentle rains awaken the soil. Till the ground for fast-growing turnips, sweet potatoes, and luscious strawberries. Wild dandelions and berries carpet the forest trail.

☀️ SUMMER · ABUNDANCE

Sun-drenched days and refreshing thunderstorms. Cultivate golden corn, juicy red tomatoes, and spicy peppers. Ocean fish teem around the sunlit coral reefs.

🍂 AUTUMN · THE HARVEST

Golden leaves and crisp mountain breezes. Harvest giant prize pumpkins, eggplants, and sweet yams. Prepare your barns and coops for the coming freeze.

❄️ WINTER · MYSTERY

A blanket of sparkling snow covers the valley fields. With crops asleep, explore deep mine shafts for rare gems, upgrade your tools, and share stories beside the tavern fire.

What Is ARC FARM?

ARC FARM is a cozy pixel-art farming life-sim inspired by Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley. You inherit your grandfather's abandoned farm and move from the city to a small valley village — and what happens next is up to you.

GOAL: turn an overgrown plot into a thriving farm across four seasons. Hoe the soil, plant seasonal seeds, water daily, harvest, and ship crops for gold. Upgrade tools, raise chickens and cows, fish the pond and the sea, mine ore, and complete daily village requests.

Along the way, greet villagers every day, give them gifts they love, and raise hearts to unlock personal stories. No game-over — just your own pace, season after season.

ONE LIVING VALLEY: a single map with no loading screens — farm, village plaza, forest pond, and beach pier. Dynamic weather, day/night cycle, seasonal crops, and villagers with daily schedules.

OWN THE HARVEST: The $ARC Token powers the valley's living economy. Cultivate prize crops, complete village board quests, and trade on-chain. Whitelist now for guaranteed presale allocation and early airdrops!

    How to Play

    WASD / arrows — walk
    Shift (hold) — run
    E at water edge — fish (while holding rod)
    E / Space — use tool, talk, harvest, sleep
    1…9, X/Z, mouse wheel — select tool
    I / Tab — inventory (arrows select, E move, Backspace discard)
    G — give gift to the villager you face
    C — eat held item
    Esc — menu (Resume, Help, Settings, Quit)
    Bottom-left bubble — public chat with other online players
    F — toggle zoom (also in Settings)
