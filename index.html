What Is ARC FARM?

ARC FARM is a cozy pixel-art farming life-sim inspired by Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley. You inherit your grandfather's abandoned farm and move from the city to a small valley village — and what happens next is up to you.

GOAL: turn an overgrown plot into a thriving farm across four seasons. Hoe the soil, plant seasonal seeds, water daily, harvest, and ship crops for gold. Upgrade tools, raise chickens and cows, fish the pond and the sea, mine ore, and complete daily village requests.

Along the way, greet villagers every day, give them gifts they love, and raise hearts to unlock personal stories. No game-over — just your own pace, season after season.

ONE LIVING VALLEY: a single map with no loading screens — farm, village plaza, forest pond, and beach pier. Dynamic weather, day/night cycle, seasonal crops, and villagers with daily schedules.

    How to Play

    WASD / arrows — walk
    Shift (hold) — run
    E at water edge — fish (while holding rod)
    E / Space — use tool, talk, harvest, sleep
    1…9, X/Z, mouse wheel — select tool
    I / Tab — inventory (arrows select, E move, Backspace discard)
    G — give gift to the villager you face
    C — eat held item
    Esc — menu (Resume, Help, Settings, Quit)
    Bottom-left bubble — public chat with other online players
    F — toggle zoom (also in Settings)

    About the Game

    ARC FARM is a browser-based village life-sim, inspired by Harvest Moon and Stardew Valley. One valley, no loading screens, progress saved on your device.

    Engine: pure JavaScript + Canvas 2D, 32px tiles. Version 0.7 — full valley, farming, animals, weather, shops and tool upgrades, background music, and pause menu with settings.
